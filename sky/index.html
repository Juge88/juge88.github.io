    HelpLights, install, trouble

    Put it on your phone’s home screen. Open this page, then use your browser’s menu: “Add to Home Screen” (Android) or Share, then “Add to Home Screen” (iPhone). It opens like an app.

    Away from home? The online app works anywhere. The sky, planets and satellites don’t need the pointer or even the internet. When the pointer is plugged in and on its Wi-Fi, the app can move it from anywhere.

    Pointer not answering? Make sure it’s plugged in and on its Wi-Fi. On its own page, your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the pointer and that the pointer has power from its USB-C cable (a computer’s USB port can be too weak). Then tap Try again.

    Arm in the wrong spot? Open “Line it up” and tap North mark. If it’s off, try the two Reverse switches, then use Trim for the last few degrees.

    Compass wrong? Close the case up, run the compass calibration, and keep metal and magnets away while you do.
